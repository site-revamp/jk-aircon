# JK Aircon Specialist Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

JK Aircon Specialist sells, installs, services, and repairs air-conditioning systems for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors compare systems and installation packages, find the right maintenance or repair service, and make an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make installation packages easier to compare.** The existing listings leave customers to work out what each price covers. The proposed layout would group Mitsubishi Electric and Daikin packages by brand and configuration, with exact model numbers, specifications, piping and installation inclusions, additional charges, and promotion and warranty terms. It would identify the HDB packages that include standard installation and direct private-property customers to request a separate quotation.
2. **Give sales, installation, servicing, and repairs distinct sections.** Visitors currently have to sort through a broad range of offerings. The proposed layout would explain when services such as chemical wash, overhaul, steam cleaning, or troubleshooting are appropriate and place a direct enquiry link beside each option.
3. **Make warranties and contact details easier to verify.** Genuine installation and servicing photos and verified customer reviews would help visitors assess the business before booking. The homepage would explain the advertised three-year workmanship warranty and product warranty terms separately once confirmed. The redesign would also make business hours and WhatsApp easier to find, reconcile the outdated address on the privacy page, and use confirmed public contact details.

## Design Improvements

Consistent package cards would align model details, installation inclusions, and quotation actions for quick comparison. Distinct service sections and concise descriptions would help visitors find the right option and enquire from the same section.
